# Page list (provisional, from real coverage)

Mike delegated the page list to Claude (2026-11-22). Status: PROVISIONAL. It comes from the real camera registry, not from keyword research. Replace it when Mike's keyword research arrives.

Rules used to choose it:
- A city page exists only if the registry holds at least 3 cameras for that city. The 10 cities with the most cameras are picked (ties broken A to Z). Rule lives in `src/lib/pages.mjs` (`curatedCities`).
- Silo levels (country level added 2026-11-23, following Argos's country -> region/city pattern but with our silo gate): world hub -> country hub `/cameras/<country>/` (united-states, canada) -> region hub `/cameras/<country>/<region>/` -> city page `/cameras/<country>/<region>/<city>/`. The region -> country table is `REGION_COUNTRY` in `ops/importers/lib/registry.mjs` (the one place); routes are built only by `countryRoute`, `regionRoute` and `cityRoute` in `src/lib/pages.mjs`. Road pages stay `/roads/<slug>/` because every road page is California-only (BC highways are not I-, SR- or US- roads), so a country folder would hold one country; the title and text say California, United States. Old URL -> new URL list: `docs/decisions/url-map.md`.
- A country hub exists only if the registry holds at least 3 cameras across its regions.
- A region hub exists only if the registry holds at least 3 cameras for that region. Ontario and Alberta have none yet, so they are not generated. British Columbia (DriveBC, added 2026-11-22) gets a hub and up to 5 city pages; its cities are "named places" decided by `bcPlace` in `ops/importers/lib/drivebc.mjs` (camera name plus DriveBC's own caption), and the 5 shown are the places with the most cameras, ties A to Z. Road pages stay California-only (BC highways are not I-, SR- or US- roads).
- Any page whose count is below 3 is `noindex` and left out of the sitemap (`isIndexable`, tested).
- Coverage: all 12 Caltrans districts loaded on the 2026-11-22 import (data/camera-registry/import-report.json, failedDistricts empty). An earlier note blamed a server error for districts 01-09; that was a wrong URL (unpadded district in the folder, padded in the file name), not an outage. Re-run `node ops/importers/caltrans.mjs` to widen it; the list then recomputes at the next build.

Every line carries "Beats Argos by:". The Argos pages for the same places were not opened; the claims below are about what our page contains.

## Features on every page (not pages)
- Search box in the header of every page (`src/layouts/Base.astro`, `src/lib/search-core.mjs`, built by `scripts/build-search.mjs`, rule `search-box`). Beats Argos by: it finds a city, a road under any spelling ("I 405", "I-405", "Interstate 405", "Highway 1"), a region, a country, a small town that has no page of its own, or a camera by its name, from a prebuilt static index fetched only when the box is first focused; every result states its kind, region AND country (so two towns with one name are never confused), a town with no page opens the map at its position, a camera name leads to its city or road page or to the official source and never to a thin per-camera page, and with JavaScript off the box is replaced by plain links to /cameras/ and /roads/. Argos is not claimed to lack search: that was not checked, the claim is about what ours does.
- `/map/?lat=&lon=&z=` (search result for a place with no page). Beats Argos by: the position is validated (never passed on unchecked), handed to the map engine as its verified share hash when the engine is deployed, and otherwise shown as coordinates with the three nearest city and road pages, so the link is useful before the engine exists.

## Live blocks on city pages (plan.md section 6, built 2026-11-22)
Each block is a server-rendered `<section data-block data-source data-asof>` with source link, attribution and an "Updated" time, text only. It is omitted when its licence row is not enabled (`tests/licensing/gate.mjs`), when its data is older than its max age at build, or when nothing is within 15 km of the city centre. One function (`site/src/lib/live.mjs`, `cityBlocks`) decides all of that and produces every number shown. No block appears on pages without data, so the blocks present differ per city.
- `road-conditions` (max age 15 min) Beats Argos by: shows only closures in effect now plus chain controls, each with its own distance from the city, delay and end time from Caltrans, instead of a flat statewide list.
- `message-signs` (15 min) Beats Argos by: the exact sign wording with the time the message was set, and a warning that a sign can keep an old message, so an old message is never passed off as current.
- `road-weather` (60 min) Beats Argos by: stations on the nearby roads with the reading time, and an honest note that Caltrans does not state the units; stations that stopped reporting are dropped.
- `forecast` (180 min) Beats Argos by: the next 48 hours from the National Weather Service on the same page as the cameras and road data, with source link and fetch time.
- `weather-alerts` (180 min) Beats Argos by: official active alerts for the city point beside the road conditions, hidden when none are active rather than shown as an empty box.
- `wildfires` (California city pages, 360 min = 6 h, NIFC WFIGS current perimeters, US public domain row in the licence file; fetched by `ops/importers/wildfire.mjs`, decided by the same `cityBlocks` function; omitted when nothing is within 50 km) Beats Argos by: lists up to 5 fires by distance from the nearest edge of the perimeter (shown as about N km), with acres, percent contained, perimeter update time and a plain InciWeb link, on the same page as the cameras and road conditions, and says fire perimeters change by the hour and to check your state road status; it never says a road is closed or safe and it disappears when the file is over 6 hours old instead of showing an old fire as current.
- `feed-freshness` (region page) Beats Argos by: one line per live feed with its last fetch time and which Caltrans districts failed to load.
- `bc-events` (British Columbia city pages, 15 min, DriveBC Open511 under the Open Government Licence - BC; fetched by `ops/importers/drivebc.mjs`, one function `cityBlocks` decides it like the California blocks) Beats Argos by: shows only events in effect now within 15 km of the place's cameras, each with DriveBC's own wording, road, start, end and last-update time (UTC) and the licence line, and is left out rather than shown empty or stale; the California-only blocks (Caltrans, NWS) never appear on BC pages and no forecast is shown because no Canadian weather licence has been read.
- `health` (24 h, our own checker, `ops/health/check.mjs`; counts from one function, `site/src/lib/health.mjs`) Beats Argos by: a checked status next to every camera link (a 200 that returns an HTML or JSON page counts as unavailable, a picture whose server says it was last modified over 6 hours ago is stale), counts per city and per road, and the time of the last check; the statuses are hidden, never shown as current, when the last run is older than 24 hours or did not finish, and the checker keeps last changed apart from status so a still picture is not called dead. Argos shows no per-camera health.

## Road pages (built 2026-11-22)
Roads: the 10 with the most cameras, each at least 3 (`curatedRoads` in `src/lib/pages.mjs`, provisional like the cities; one spelling per road through `roadSlug` in `ops/importers/lib/registry.mjs`). Cameras are ordered by position along the road's line of best fit (`src/lib/roads.mjs`), not by postmile or milepost. Closures, chain controls and signs come from the same feeds and the same licence and freshness gates as the city blocks (`roadBlocks` in `src/lib/live.mjs`), matched by `roadSlug`, each with its 3 nearest cameras of that road within 25 km (`nearestCameras`); an item with no camera that close is counted, not listed.
- `cameras` (road page, registry) Beats Argos by: every camera on the road in driving order per direction instead of a list by city, with a checked status beside each.
- `road-cities` (registry) Beats Argos by: the cities along the road with camera counts and links to their pages; where a road passes no city with a page, the nearest city pages with their distance.
- `road-conditions`, `message-signs` (15 min) Beats Argos by: each closure, chain control and sign carries its nearest cameras with the distance in km, so a driver can look at the spot; Argos has no event-to-camera link.
- `road-weather` (60 min), `weather-alerts` (180 min) Beats Argos by: road weather stations on this road and the National Weather Service alerts for the cities listed, with fetch times, on the road page; omitted when stale or unlicensed.
- `wildfires` (road page, 360 min, same file and gates as the city block, measured from the road's cameras) Beats Argos by: the fires whose perimeter edge is within 50 km of any camera on this road, nearest first, each with acres, percent contained and update time, beside the road's closures and cameras; omitted when none are that close or the file is stale.
- `coverage` (registry) Beats Argos by: the largest gap in km between neighbouring cameras per direction, so a driver knows where there is no camera to look at; Argos shows no coverage gaps.
Not built: CHP incidents and Canadian 511 (no licence row or importer yet).
