# WSDOT: written permission request (drafted 2026-11-24, NOT SENT)

To: the WSDOT travel data contact (find the address on https://wsdot.wa.gov/travel/real-time/travel-data or the Travel Center API page before sending; none read yet)
Subject: Question: listing WSDOT traffic camera links on an advertising-supported directory

Hello,

I run World Traffic Cameras (worldtrafficcameras.com), a free public directory of traffic camera links with a free map. It shows ads beside the data, so use is commercial. We do not copy, host, resize or change any camera picture: each camera name is a link to the official WSDOT picture address from the public Travel Center camera list (data.wsdot.wa.gov/travelcenter/Cameras.json). We never charge users.

The travel information disclaimer (wsdot.wa.gov/about/policies/travel-information-disclaimer) covers warranties and does not say whether the camera list and picture links may be republished. Please confirm in writing:
1. Whether listing the camera names, locations and links to your own picture addresses on an advertising-supported site is permitted.
2. The credit wording you want.
3. Any request limit we must keep (we read the list about once every 3 hours and check each picture address at most 4 times a second).

We will credit WSDOT on every page that shows these cameras and honour your limits.

Thank you.
