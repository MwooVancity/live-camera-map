Subject: Question: camera locations and links from the national road network on an advertising-supported directory

Hello,

I run World Traffic Cameras (worldtrafficcameras.com), a free public directory of traffic camera links with a free map. It shows ads beside the data, so use is commercial. We do not copy, host, resize or cache camera pictures: each camera entry only links to the official page.

Your data re-use licence and the DATEX II publications describe traffic data, but I could not find a camera list or camera links among them. Please could you tell me:
1. Whether the state road network (DIR) cameras are published as a catalogue (names, coordinates, stable links) and where.
2. Whether listing those names, locations and links on an advertising-supported site is permitted, and under which licence.
3. The credit wording you want, and any request limit we must keep.

We will credit Bison Futé on every page that shows these cameras and honour your limits.
