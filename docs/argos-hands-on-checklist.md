# Argos hands-on checklist (for Mike, in your own browser)

Use their site the way any visitor would. Send Claude screenshots plus one line each. Skip anything that asks for payment. Signing in means accepting their terms: your choice, not needed for the rest.
Do not send their text, pictures or files for us to copy; we only want what the feature does.

| # | Try this | Tell us |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the home page on your phone | What do you see first? Anything confusing or slow? |
| 2 | Search your own city | Did you find a camera? How many taps? |
| 3 | Open one camera | Sign-in, ad or limit? Is the picture current? |
| 4 | Open the map | What appears before you can use it? |
| 5 | Switch on two or three map layers | Which ones would you come back for? |
| 6 | Click a plane or a ship | What details show? |
| 7 | Open a country "today" page | What is on it that ours lacks? |
| 8 | Try the menu, share and save | What works well? |
| 9 | Note one thing that annoyed you | We make sure ours does not do it. |
| 10 | Do 1 to 4 on worldtrafficcameras.com | Where is theirs better, and where is ours? |
