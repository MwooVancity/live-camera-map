# World hunt 2, 2026-11-24 (Switzerland, Hungary, Indonesia, Croatia, Greece, Slovakia, Sweden, Brazil, South Africa, Slovenia, Belgium, Thailand)

Method: WebSearch (standard), WebFetch of official pages, plus a few curl calls (CKAN and Opendatasoft search APIs, one gov.si xlsx download, robots.txt files). User-Agent `worldtrafficcameras-research`, no key, no login, no form, at most one request per second. VERIFIED = I opened the official page or endpoint and saw it. UNVERIFIED = search snippet or third party. "Could not open" = fetch failed or gave no text. No Argos, gev/ or research-private/ read. Picture HEAD requests: 0 (no source gave a picture URL I could test). Searches were in English plus some local-language terms (Swedish, Slovenian, Croatian, Hungarian, Slovak, Thai, Portuguese, Indonesian, Dutch, German); Greek and Slovak local-language coverage was thin.

Result in one line: no country gave an open list with names, coordinates and picture links that I could count. The best lead is Sweden (Trafikverket, CC0 licence page opened, but a free account is needed). Slovenia has an official list of 649 sheet rows but no coordinates.
