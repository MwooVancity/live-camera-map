## Verdict

READY for locations plus link-out to the official picture URL (our launch mode). Why it passes the READY test: official owner; no key; licence CC BY SA 4.0 permits commercial reuse with attribution; robots does not block the paths used; stable picture URLs; coordinates present.

Caveats that must stay on the row (not conflicts, but limits):
1. CC BY SA is on the dataset record (locations, addresses, links). The SIMM web pages (camaras.html, /simm/camaras-de-circuito-cerrado) carry no separate terms for the JPEG files (VERIFIED, no licence text found). So link out only; do NOT embed, proxy or cache the pictures (CLAUDE.md hard rule) until the image permission is confirmed by Mike.
2. ShareAlike: a derived database of these points must be offered under CC BY SA 4.0. Keep the Medellin points in their own file with the attribution sentence, never merged into a differently licensed file without a note.
3. Layer field `link_foto` for objectid 7, 26, 34 and 74-81 is unusable; skip them (camera ids must be stable: use `medellin-simm-<objectid>` or the imagenNN number; the number is the stable key because objectid is a database id).
4. The Medellin 2026 circular named in the record (C_SGMED_0054_2026) sits under a robots-disallowed path and was not read; it may restate the open-data policy.
