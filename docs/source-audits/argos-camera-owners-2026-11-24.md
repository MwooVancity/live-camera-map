Also on the same page (not cameras, kept as our lead list for later pages): their globe code and wind layer are adapted from God's Eye View under the MIT licence (their words: frame governor, atmosphere, trackpad zoom, camera icon, aircraft classification, imagery sharpening, event imagery, street-level viewer, particle wind layer). So Argos's map core is a GEV derivative: what GEV does, Argos's free map mostly does; Argos's own additions are the geopolitics, finance, VIP and risk layers and the paid tools (see docs/source-audits/argos-feature-inventory-2026-11-24.md).
