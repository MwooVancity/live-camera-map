## Why HOLD, not READY
1. Licence: the only canton-wide text prohibits public or commercial reuse without written consent (ads count as commercial). Rule: conflicting or restrictive = not READY.
2. No coordinates in the source.
3. Count 12 verified, 36 unverified.
Link-out only (a link to the official page, no image embed) is the only thing that could ship without consent, and that still lacks coordinates, so no build now.
