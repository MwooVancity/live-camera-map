# World hunt 3, 2026-11-24 (remaining US states and Canadian provinces)

Method: one ArcGIS Hub dataset search (hub.arcgis.com/api/v3/datasets, plus about 24 per-state searches at 1 per second), then for each lead one count query, one sample record, one layer metadata read, one HEAD on one picture URL, robots.txt of the hosts. WebSearch (standard) for the key-gated states. User-Agent `worldtrafficcameras-research`, no key, no login, no form. VERIFIED = I opened or queried it. UNVERIFIED = search snippet or a third-party copy. No Argos, gev/ or research-private/ read. No bot check or 403 hit on any camera or list host (services.arcgis.com/robots.txt returns 403 "Invalid URL" for every client; that is the ArcGIS REST host, not a bot check on the data, noted below).

Already in our registry (skipped): Caltrans, Austin, Toronto, Calgary, DriveBC, Quebec, Iceland, Finland, Hong Kong, London, NSW, plus alberta-511 and ontario-511 rows. Closed earlier (skipped): WSDOT, Oregon, Maryland, Florida, NYC, Houston, TxDOT, DelDOT, King County, Ontario 511.
